# Photo shot list — Mac Auto & Collision website

Shoot everything **landscape (wide)**, in daylight or with the shop lights all on, phone held level. Wipe the lens first. Any modern phone is fine. Don't crop or filter; the site handles sizing.

## Must-have (these go live first)

| # | Shot | Where it's used | Notes |
|---|------|-----------------|-------|
| 1 | **Storefront with the sign**, from across the lot, morning light | Hero background, Google Business, social | Include the building, sign and a clean stretch of lot. Move trash cans and cones out of frame. |
| 2 | **Alan (owner) in front of the shop or a finished car** | About section | Shoulders up or waist up, looking at camera, clean shirt with the shop name if you have one. |
| 3 | **Whole crew together** in a bay | About section | Everyone in uniform if possible. Even 3 people looks great. |
| 4 | **Before / After pair #1** (a visible collision repair) | Gallery, hero card | Same angle, same distance, same spot for both photos. Before shot BEFORE anything is removed. |
| 5 | **Before / After pair #2** | Gallery | A different vehicle, different kind of damage (rear-end, side, hail, rust). |
| 6 | **Before / After pair #3** | Gallery | Ideally a truck, since trucks are common here. |
| 7 | **Paint booth with a vehicle inside**, lights on | Services: Paint & Refinishing | Shoot through the door or from inside with a tech spraying (in mask). |
| 8 | **Frame machine / measuring** with a vehicle on it | Services: Frame & Structural | Shows the capability chains and dealers claim small shops don't have. |
| 9 | **Front office / waiting area** | Contact section | Tidy the counter. Customers want to know what walking in feels like. |

## For each Before / After pair
- Take the **Before** the day the car arrives, before teardown.
- Take the **After** in the same spot, same angle, after the final wash.
- Note the year/make/model and what was repaired. That becomes the caption.
