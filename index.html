How it works

From wreck to road in four steps

Accidents are stressful enough. The repair shouldn't be. Here's what to expect when you bring your vehicle to us.

Get an estimate

Drive in during business hours or send us photos. We write a clear, itemized estimate at no charge and no obligation.

We handle the claim

Give us your claim number and we coordinate with the adjuster, negotiate the repair plan and order OEM or quality parts.

Repair & refinish

Body, frame, mechanical and paint work done in-house. We keep you updated and call you if anything changes.

Inspect & drive home

Every repair is checked, cleaned and reviewed with you before you pick it up. We stand behind our work.

Insurance claims

Know your rights after an accident

Your insurance company may recommend a shop, but the choice is yours. Here's how we make the claim easier.

  • We work with every insurance carrierState Farm, Allstate, Progressive, GEICO, USAA, Alfa and the rest. If your policy covers it, we can repair it.
  • One estimate is all you needAlabama does not require multiple estimates. Bring us the claim and we'll take it from there.
  • We handle supplementsIf we find hidden damage once the vehicle is apart, we document it and work it out with the adjuster, not you.
  • Paying out of pocket?No claim, no problem. We'll give you an honest price and repair options that fit your budget.

What to bring

Your driver's license, insurance card, claim number if you have one, and the vehicle. That's it. We take care of the rest.
